# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mainly organisers and members of offices and classes running a gift exchange, on phones. Friends and families use it too. Organisers track a bigger group and need to chase people; members open one link from a group chat.

## Product Purpose

Draw names for a gift exchange from one shared link. Success: everyone gets their person, and nobody else can see who drew whom.

## Positioning

The organiser never receives the results. Each person picks their own name, sets a PIN and unwraps who they drew, and Santa and their person can message anonymously.

## Operating Context

Set up in a minute, shared in a group chat, then opened repeatedly in the weeks before a party date. No accounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

English only. Budgets are in baht. Wish lists recognise Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop links, but shops block fetching product details. Names and wishes people type can be in any language, including Thai.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Secret Santa. The user pointed to the Instagram page of Cultural Fest 2027 as the style they like. Its logo, photos and wording belong to the festival and must not be reused.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, usage numbers or customers exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

- The secret comes first: never put a result on screen by surprise.
- Works for someone who opened one link and has never seen the site.
- The organiser can chase people without learning anything private.
