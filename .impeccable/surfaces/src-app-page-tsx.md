Scope: whole site. Mode: Operate, with a Persuade home page. User-pinned reference replaces the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gift exchange should feel like the poster for a Christmas fair, a collage laid over a night of lights, and not like a tidy app of rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Dark night with blurred gold, red and green lights. Gold script headings. Torn cream paper notes carry all content. Red and green tickets with punched edges act as buttons and labels. Die-cut stickers, gift tags and calendar date chips. No floating chrome.

STORY: A visitor sees the name and the promise, starts a draw, shares a link. A member picks their name and unwraps a gift to find a name on a tag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Huge gold script Secret Santa inside a thin gold frame, a die-cut gift sticker, a red ticket with the promise, a paper note, and the Start a draw ticket above the fold on a phone.

FORM: Pinned by the user: the style of the Cultural Fest 2027 Instagram page. Own artwork only. Seed key: none, user-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
